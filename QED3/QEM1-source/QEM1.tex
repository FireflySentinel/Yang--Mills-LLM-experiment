\begin{abstract}
We construct non-Gaussian laws of closed two-form fields from noncompact lattice QED with chamber fermions. Their Schwinger functions satisfy the Osterwalder--Schrader axioms, including clustering. The bare mass is chosen independently of the volume. Almost every translation-ergodic component of every volume-first limit has these properties. For each compact probe with a positive free fourth coefficient, its fourth cumulant is positive at sufficiently small charge.
\end{abstract}
