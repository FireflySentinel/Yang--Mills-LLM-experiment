This repo contains slop in progress. We are trying to turn Claude onto problems in constructive quantum field theory, including QED3, YM3, and, we hope, YM4. 
We are beginning with QED3 and YM3, where the UV situation is friendlier. Some of the ideas will be applicable (perhaps in a more complicated form) to YM4, and we will post the model's progress (or apparent progress).
We will digest/exposit and formalize what we can. For example, we are currently working on a human-written exposition of one of Claude's arguments, a self-contained theorem that applies to all of the theories above.
